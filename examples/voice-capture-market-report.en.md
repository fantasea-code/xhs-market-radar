## 1. Executive Summary

- **Direction**: Validated. The first version should be positioned as a "low-friction quick capture tool / life context capture", NOT a "24h full-time recording device".
- **Fake Demand?**: No. Dedicated AI wearables like Looki, Omi, and SwitchBot MindClip prove that "life streaming / passive memory capture" is a real direction. The real risks lie in privacy, battery life, output quality, price, and workflow integration.
- **Why not 24h recording?**: Strong evidence supports "low-friction capture + auto-organization + review value", NOT "users wanting to store 24h of raw audio". 24h recording brings massive friction regarding privacy, battery, storage, and ASR costs.
- **Top User Pain Points**: Fleeting inspiration, organizing voice memos is tedious, phone/watch app launch is too slow, inaccurate ASR, scattered long-term data.
- **Existing Solutions**: Looki, Omi, Plaud / AI Voice Cards, Apple Watch + Flash Notes, iPhone Shortcuts, DJI Mic DIY, SwitchBot MindClip.
- **Our Opportunity**: Camera-less, low-cost, physical button, local caching, non-meeting scenarios, Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) / low subscription pressure.

- **Apple Watch**: 2100+ likes on ADHD capture posts. Huge demand for quick, frictionless input.

## 6. Internal Calibration

### Should Do
- Position as "life/inspiration/todo capture", not "meeting recorder".
- Highlight: camera-less, physical button, long battery, local cache, BYOK, open export.
- Demo 30-sec video: Phone unlock vs. One-button hardware capture.

### Should NOT Do
- Do not compete with Plaud for "meeting summaries".
- Do not add a camera in V1.
- Do not rely entirely on recurring subscriptions.
